# Project Overview

## Project Ownership and Purpose

This project is developed for SOS Infocity, aimed at providing a comprehensive web solution for showcasing their services, achievements, and organizational values. The website serves as a platform for:

- Highlighting SOS Infocity's impact across various sectors.
- Providing detailed information about their services and solutions.
- Engaging with potential clients and partners through clear service and contact information.

## Detailed Page Overview

### Home Page
- **Purpose**: Introduces SOS Infocity and provides an overview of their services.
- **Key Features**: Hero banner and quick links to services.

### About Page
- **Purpose**: Shares the history, mission, and vision of SOS Infocity.
- **Key Features**: Timeline of achievements, team introduction, and organizational values.

### Impact Page
- **Purpose**: Highlights the impact of SOS Infocity's work across various sectors.
- **Key Features**: Sector-specific case studies, testimonials, and a gallery.

### Services Pages
- **Purpose**: Provides detailed information about each service offered by SOS Infocity.
- **Pages Included**:
  - AI Intelligent Solutions
  - Connectivity Solutions
  - Data BI Analytics
  - IT Network Solutions
  - Security Surveillance
  - Software Engineering
- **Key Features**: Service descriptions, benefits, and client success stories.

### Life at SOS Page
- **Purpose**: Showcases the work culture and employee experiences at SOS Infocity.
- **Key Features**: Employee testimonials, gallery, and career opportunities.

### Contact Page
- **Purpose**: Enables visitors to get in touch with SOS Infocity.
- **Key Features**: Contact form and office locations.

## Project Structure

The project is organized as follows:

- **HTML Pages**: Located in the `pages/` directory, including `about.html`, `impact.html`, etc.
- **CSS Files**: Stylesheets are in `assets/css/`, with plugins in `assets/css/plugins/`.
- **JavaScript Files**: Scripts are in `assets/js/`, with plugins in `assets/js/plugins/`.
- **Images**: Stored in `assets/images/` and subdirectories for organization.
- **SCSS Files**: Source styles are in `assets/scss/`.
